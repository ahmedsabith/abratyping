# ABRA Businessmen Services — Animated Single-Page Website

## Open locally
1. Extract the ZIP to a folder.
2. Open `index.html` in a modern browser, or serve the folder with a local static server for the most realistic preview.
3. Keep the `assets` folder alongside `index.html`.

## Included
- Responsive, single-page ABRA Businessmen Services website.
- Bold but professional entrance and scroll-reveal animations.
- Service-card reveal and hover effects; service cards remain informational and non-clickable.
- Animated sticky header, button interactions, and floating WhatsApp control.
- Enquiry form that validates the required fields and opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message to +971 54 510 4716. The visitor must review and send the message in WhatsApp.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` support for visitors who prefer less motion.

## Before publishing
- Replace the clearly labelled sample testimonials with genuine customer feedback and obtain permission to publish it.
- Confirm all business details, service descriptions, and contact information.
- The logo is included in `assets/abra-logo.png`.
